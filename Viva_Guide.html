ERF Framework: Final Project Viva Guide

This guide is designed to help Ali Naqi and Muhammad Ahmad defend their project during the Q&A session. It covers every technical detail and concept used.

1. Core Project Overview

Q: What is the main goal of your project? We replicated and then "improvised" the Enhanced Random Forest (ERF) framework from the 2025 paper by Hussain et al. Our goal was to prove that by using academic Data Science techniques (IQR, PCA, K-Fold), we could scale the model to a much larger dataset while achieving superior 92% F1-Scores.

2. Data Science Concepts (The "Marks" Section)

Q: Why did you use IQR (Interquartile Range)? The raw App Store dataset is noisy. An app with a $999 price or 10 million reviews is an outlier. We used the mathematical IQR method (Q3 - Q1) to identify and remove these extreme outliers, cleaning the distribution for our classifiers.
Q: What is the benefit of PCA in this project? Our feature engineering created 46 different variables. Many of these might be correlated. PCA (Principal Component Analysis) reduced this down to the top 24 components that retain 95% of the variance, making the models faster and preventing the "Curse of Dimensionality."
Q: Why Stratified K-Fold instead of a simple split? A simple 80/20 split can be "lucky." Stratified K-Fold (k=3) ensures that every model is tested 3 times on different data subsets while maintaining the class balance (High vs Low rating). Our reported 85% Accuracy is the average of these folds, making it scientifically reliable.

Q: How did you "rescue" Logistic Regression? In Task 2, LR failed with 0.43 F1. We rescued it to 0.92 F1 by using Log Transforms (to handle skewness) and Feature Interaction (like Reviews per Day). This proves that with good feature engineering, simple models can perform as well as complex ensembles.

4. Models & Ensemble Approach (The "New" Model)

Q: Did you add any new models beyond what the paper used? Yes! While the paper used standalone Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost, we introduced a Soft Voting Ensemble Classifier.
Q: How does your Soft Voting Ensemble work? Instead of relying on one model, the Ensemble runs LR, RF, and XGBoost simultaneously. Rather than just picking a 'High' or 'Low' class (Hard Voting), it averages the predicted probabilities / confidence scores of all three models (Soft Voting). This combines the unique strengths of each algorithm, leading to maximum stability.

5. Handling "Negative" Results

Q: Why did SMOTE fail in your project? Our ablation study showed SMOTE decreased performance. This is because our class imbalance (85:15) was manageable after IQR cleaning. SMOTE created "synthetic" samples that blurred the decision boundary, adding noise rather than value. We documented this as a Negative Finding, which is a sign of honest, rigorous research.

5. Technical Hurdles

Q: What was the biggest technical challenge? Processing 1.2 Million rows on a laptop. We solved this by optimizing data types, dropping irrelevant columns early, and using n_jobs=-1 for parallel processing during Random Forest training.

6. Simple Roman Urdu Explanations (For Teacher Q&A)

Q: Dataset kya hai aur Target variable kya hai? Yeh Apple App Store ka data hai jisme 1.2 million apps ki information thi (size, price, genre, reviews). Humne sirf woh 57,862 apps rakhi jo 100 se zyada reviews rakhti thein. Humein predict karna tha ke kisi app ki rating 'High' (4.0 se upar) hogi ya 'Low'.
Q: Original Paper mein kya masla tha? Original paper ne sirf 7,197 apps use ki thein jo poore data ka sirf 0.6% tha. Itne kam data par model asli duniya mein kaam nahi karta aur sirf 'ratta' lagata hai (overfitting). Unhone outlier removal ya hyperparameter tuning bhi nahi ki thi.
Q: ERF (Extra Random Forest) kya hai? Normal Random Forest har tree mein best split dhundta hai, jabke ERF (Extra Randomized Forest) har tree mein random split karta hai. Is wajah se ERF zyada fast hota hai aur baday data par better generalize karta hai (overfitting se bachata hai). Simple misaal: 100 log kuch soch ke jawab dain aur kuch randomly soch kar, toh nayay angles samne aate hain.
Q: Aapne konsay 3 base models use kiye aur kyun? 1. Logistic Regression: Baseline model. Fast aur simple. Humne prove kiya ke data saaf ho toh simple model bhi 92% F1 de sakta hai.
2. Random Forest (ERF): Outliers se protected aur complex data patterns ko capture karta hai bina overfit kiye.
3. XGBoost: Sab se advanced model jo 'boosting' use karta hai (har agla tree pichle ki galtiyan theek karta hai). Isne sab se accurate score (0.9209 F1) diya.
Q: Hyperparameter Tuning kya hoti hai aur aapne konsi use ki? Tuning aise hai jaise chai banate waqt cheeni aur patti set karna. Humne RandomizedSearchCV use ki. Humne Parameters ki range di (jaise n_estimators 100 se 300) aur model ne randomly combinations try kar ke best combination select ki. GridSearch use nahi kiya kyunke wo bohot slow hota hai.
Q: SMOTE (Negative Finding) ka Roman Urdu jawab: SMOTE minority class ke artificial (fake) samples banata hai. Humne try kiya lekin F1 score 0.92 se gir kar 0.78 ho gaya. Iski wajah yeh thi ke PCA hone ke baad compressed data mein SMOTE ne ajeeb jagahon par samples bana diye jo model ko confuse kar rahe thay. Isay 'Negative Finding' ke tor par batana hamari scientific honesty ko show karta hai.
